- Project 2: Google Sheets Analysis -

For this project, I analysed the most streamed songs of all time. I used a dataset from the online website, ‘Kaggle’, which I downloaded and imported to Google Sheets to analyse and created a dashboard to visualise the data. The downloaded file from ‘Kaggle’ was very large and took some time to analyse and make sense of all the data on the sheet. Each song had a detailed data analysis which included interesting data sets like the BPM (beats per minute) of the song and the key of the song.

When creating the charts for the dashboard, some of them were quite ambitious and difficult to make. One of them in particular, the “Songs Released Per Year” pie chart, was challenging because the data was spread out on the sheet and needed to be condensed and counted separately. At first, the pie chart had also been not proportional when I thought I had done everything right before. To overcome this, I worked with a classmate to count the data and create a cohesive pie chart together, where we found that we had to put the correct settings for the chart to create the final pie chart.

In the end, despite all the challenges faced to analyse a large set of data, I am still quite proud of my work even though it doesn’t look as good as I pictured it to be. In the future, I hope to continue to enhance my analytical and visualisation skills by creating more polished and insightful dashboards.
